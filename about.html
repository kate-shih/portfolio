hi, i'm kate!

I’m currently a senior at The University of Texas at Austin double majoring in Informatics and Design.

My design journey started in high school when I joined the school paper. What began as a love for layout design (my hot take is that InDesign is the best Adobe product) quickly turned into a love for designing digital interfaces.

I enjoy learning about the people I’m designing for and finding interesting, creative ways to solve the problems they face. When I’m not interviewing users or poking at pixels on my screen, you can probably find me watching a movie, reading, or experimenting on the letterpress.
